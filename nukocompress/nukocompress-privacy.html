Privacy Policy for NukoCompress

Last updated: 15 Sep 2026

NukoCompress ("the app") is an image compression app for Android, developed by Nuko ("we", "our"). This page explains what data the app does — and does not — collect.

Short version

NukoCompress does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All image compression happens entirely on your device — your photos and files are never uploaded to any server. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs.

What the app does with your files

NukoCompress reads and writes files only when you explicitly ask it to, through Android's built-in image and folder pickers (the Storage Access Framework). Selected images are compressed entirely on your device and written to the destination you choose (your gallery or the original folder). The app does not scan, index, or access any files you have not directly chosen through those pickers, and it never uploads any image or file anywhere.

Permissions

NukoCompress requests the following permissions, for the purposes below:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: used only to show compression progress (e.g. how many images are done) as a notification. Notifications are generated entirely on-device and never sent anywhere.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PROCESSING: used so compression can keep running when you switch away from the app. This permission is unrelated to sending any file anywhere.
  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE / com.android.vending.BILLING: the app includes Google's Play Billing Library, and these permissions are used only by that library to communicate with Google Play for an optional in-app purchase (a Pro upgrade). They are never used to send your images or files anywhere. The purchase flow itself is handled entirely by Google Play, and payment details never reach us.

Data collection and analytics

We do not collect:

  • Personal information (name, email, location, etc.)
  • Usage analytics or crash reports sent to any server
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Your image/photo files themselves (never transmitted off your device)
  • Any data at all, from anyone, including children

Standard Android system logs generated during a crash stay on your device and are only visible to you (or to us if you voluntarily choose to send them to us, e.g. by email, when reporting a bug).
