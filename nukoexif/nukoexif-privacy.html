Privacy Policy for NukoExif

Last updated: 20 Sep 2026

NukoExif ("the app") is a photo metadata (EXIF) viewer and remover for Android, developed by Nuko ("we", "our"). This page explains what data the app does — and does not — collect.

Short version

NukoExif does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Reading and removing a photo's location (GPS), date/time, camera details and other metadata happens entirely on your device — your photos and their metadata are never uploaded to any server. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs.

What the app does with your files

NukoExif reads photos only when you explicitly ask it to, through Android's built-in photo picker and folder picker (the Storage Access Framework). A selected photo is copied to the app's private temporary storage on your device, and the metadata is removed from that copy — your original photo is never modified. The processed copy is saved to your device's gallery (Pictures/NukoExif) or handed to the app you choose only when you tap "Save to gallery" or "Share". The app does not scan, index, or access any photos you have not directly chosen through those pickers, and it never uploads any photo or file anywhere.

About location data

The app does not request location permission and never accesses your device's current location. The location shown on screen is only the GPS metadata already recorded inside the photo you selected. Only if you tap "Open in Maps" are those coordinates handed to the map app on your device.

Permissions

NukoExif declares the following permissions, for the purposes below:

  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE / com.android.vending.BILLING: the app includes Google's Play Billing Library, and these permissions are used only by that library to communicate with Google Play for an optional future in-app purchase (a Pro upgrade). They are never used to send your photos or metadata anywhere. The purchase flow itself is handled entirely by Google Play, and payment details never reach us.

Data collection and analytics

We do not collect:

  • Personal information (name, email, location, etc.)
  • Usage analytics or crash reports sent to any server
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Your photo files or their metadata themselves (never transmitted off your device)
  • Any data at all, from anyone, including children

Removal presets you save in the app (your choice of which categories to remove) are stored only in the app's private storage on your device and are never sent anywhere. Standard Android system logs generated during a crash stay on your device and are only visible to you (or to us if you voluntarily choose to send them to us, e.g. by email, when reporting a bug).

Third-party services

The only third-party service NukoExif integrates is Google's Play Billing Library, used for in-app purchases. It does not include any analytics platform, ad network, or cloud storage service. For how Google Play handles information during a purchase, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 (or the relevant age in your country).

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
